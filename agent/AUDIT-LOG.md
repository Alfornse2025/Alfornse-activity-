# AUDIT LOG

Append-only record of everything the Chief of Staff agent proposed, and what happened to it. Corrections are new lines, never edits. Times are EAT.

**Log an entry for:** every draft created, every calendar change proposed, every outbound action proposed, every SENSITIVE flag, every escalation, every approval granted or refused.
**Do not log:** the content of SENSITIVE items — the log records that an item was flagged and what was decided, never the material itself.

Columns: `Date` · `Time` · `Bucket/Class` · `Item` · `Proposed` · `State` · `Outcome`
State: `Proposed` → `Approved` / `Refused` / `Withdrawn` → `Done`.
