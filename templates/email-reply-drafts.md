# EMAIL DRAFT TEMPLATES

Every draft is written in Alfornse's voice (`agent/MEMORY.md §4`), created in Gmail Drafts, and **never sent**. Three tones are offered so the choice takes seconds rather than a rewrite.

**Tone selection:** *short* for internal and people you speak to weekly · *detailed* when the reply itself is the deliverable or a record is needed · *diplomatic* for external counterparties, senior officials, and anything where a "no" has to survive the relationship.

---

## 1 · Reply draft — the three-tone block

```
Thread: {subject}          From: {sender}          Bucket/Class: {A3 · DECIDE}
The ask: {what they want}  By: {deadline}          If missed: {consequence}
```

**Short**
> {Sender first name} — {answer in one line}. {The one thing you need from them, with a date.}
> Alfornse

**Detailed**
> Dear {Name},
>
> {Three-line summary of position: where things stand · what you propose · what it depends on.}
>
> {The substance — bullets, not paragraphs, where there is more than one point.}
>
> {The ask, with a specific date and named owner.}
>
> Regards,
> Alfornse

**Diplomatic**
> Dear {Name},
>
> Thank you for {specific thing — not "your email"}.
>
> {Acknowledge their position accurately before departing from it.} {Your position, stated once, without apology.}
>
> {A proposed path forward with a specific next step and date — never "let me know what works".}
>
> Regards,
> Alfornse

```
Confidence: {High/Medium/Low} — {what would raise it}
Need from you before this is trustworthy: {clarifying question, or "nothing"}
```

## 3 · Canned short forms

| Situation | Draft |
|---|---|
| **Holding reply** (urgent, needs thought) | "{Name} — received. I'll come back to you by {specific time}, with {what}." |
| **Chase** (waiting on someone) | "{Name} — following up on {item}, due {original date}. Is {new date} realistic? If something is blocking it, tell me what and I'll help clear it." |
| **Decline a meeting** | "{Name} — I can't make {meeting}; {reason in one clause}. {Named delegate} will attend / please send the deck and minutes. On {the one substantive point}: {position}." |
| **Accept with a condition** | "Happy to join. So the hour is useful, could we confirm {objective/paper/decision} beforehand?" |
| **Request a document** | "{Name} — could you send {document} by {date}? It's what {deliverable} is waiting on." |
| **Introduce / refer** | "{A}, meet {B}. {B} — {A} is {one line}. I'll leave you two to it." |
| **Hard no, relationship intact** | "Thank you for thinking of me — I'm not able to take this on {this quarter / at all}, and I'd rather say so now than hold it up. {Alternative, if there is a real one.}" |

## 4 · Rules for every draft

- Subject lines under 8 words, describing the ask.
- One ask per email. Two asks means two emails or a numbered list with owners.
- Dates absolute (`27 Jul`), never relative (`next Tuesday`).
- Currency as `KES 1,000,000`.
- No "Hope this finds you well", no apology for a delay that wasn't real.
- Never draft contract, negotiation or settlement language unprompted — that is SENSITIVE and stops (`agent/BOUNDARIES.md §3`).
- Never quote figures, taxpayer detail or named-individual assessments from KRA Tier-1 material.
- Every draft is logged in `agent/AUDIT-LOG.md` and stays in Drafts until explicitly approved.
