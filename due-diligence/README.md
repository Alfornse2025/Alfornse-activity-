## Conventions

Every substantive statement in these profiles carries one of three confidence markers:

- **[VERIFIED]** — corroborated against a named, checkable third-party source.
- **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]** — existence confirmed by a named authoritative source
  (a court's case index, a registry aggregator), but the underlying document was not
  retrieved in full.
- **[UNVERIFIED — reported]** — supplied from an external profile with no
  retrievable source; a lead, not a fact.

These are open-source reviews. They are **Tier 3** under the confidentiality rules in
`SYSTEM.md` — they contain no privileged or taxpayer-identifiable material, and
concern external parties only. They are not a substitute for legal, financial or
regulatory due diligence.
